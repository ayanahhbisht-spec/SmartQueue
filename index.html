SmartQueue Guide

How the Smart Queue Works

🎫 How is wait time calculated?

The system computes dynamic wait times based on average consultation durations per department, active doctors on duty, and real-time pace.

🚨 How does Priority Triage work?

Urgent requests are verified through clinical guidelines and re-slotted near the front of the queue without cancelling other patients.

⏱ What if I miss my token number?

No penalty! Simply tap In 30 min, In 2 hrs, or Tomorrow to preserve your place with minimal wait.
